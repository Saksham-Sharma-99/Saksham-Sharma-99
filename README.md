## About me

I'm Saksham Sharma, an IIT Roorkee graduate and software engineer focused on **backend infrastructure, AI-powered products, and distributed systems**.

I enjoy owning problems end to end: understanding the underlying problem, designing the architecture, building the system, finding its bottlenecks, and making it production-ready.

My work spans event-driven architectures, AI agent orchestration and memory, integration platforms, and performance engineering.

I believe in clean abstractions, systems you can reason about, and optimizations you can measure. I like understanding not just *how* software works, but *why* it works the way it does.
